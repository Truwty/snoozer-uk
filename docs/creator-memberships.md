# Creator membership offers — preview increment

Verified profile owners can create, edit, hide and remove up to five proposals with optimistic revision checks. Public previews expose only visible tiers on an independently eligible nonempty sandbox store. Prices are hypothetical monthly GBP amounts, not Stripe prices. No subscribe button, payments, member accounts, benefits access or Snoozer Plus association.

## Release and acceptance

Run full CI and deploy additive schema before the app. Verify private owner RPC grants, anonymous denial of owner data, and public projection gating. Do not mutate historical orders or provider gates. Recurring subscriptions, renewal/cancellation, failed invoices, refund/dispute lifecycle, member entitlement and tax/provider acceptance are explicitly not implemented by this preview.
